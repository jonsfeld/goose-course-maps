## Course-level / general strategy notes

### Architect intent (Forrest Richardson)

- **"The defence of the course is all about the natural terrain and the drama you get from playing up, down and around."** [confidence: **high** — direct architect quote]. Source: https://www.golfcoursearchitecture.net/content/richardson-restores-roosevelt-course-in-las-griffith-park
- **"The last thing Roosevelt needs is an abundance of bunkers to make it interesting."** [confidence: **high** — direct architect quote]. Source: Golf Course Architecture.
- Post-renovation bunker count: **only five total bunkers**, including greenside bunkers and a cross bunker on hole 4. [confidence: **high**]. Source: https://www.forrestrichardsongolf.com/city-of-los-angeles-restores-roosevelt-golf-course/
- Greens were "restored to previous configurations" and greens/tees were "enlarged/reclaimed" during renovation. [confidence: **high**]. Sources: https://www.forrestrichardsongolf.com/projects/roosevelt-golf-course-griffith-park-california/ ; GCA articles.
- **"The course doesn't make you feel as if you've hiked the Alps, yet you become totally lost in the hills."** [confidence: **high** — direct architect quote]. Source: https://www.golfcoursearchitecture.net/content/roosevelt-course-reopens-following-richardson-restoration
- Bunker system: **Better Billy Bunker** installed. [confidence: **high**]. Source: GCA article.
- "Dramatic hills and valleys that form the heart and strategy of the holes." [confidence: **high**]. Source: GCA article.

### Course character
- **Walking only** — no carts, no cart paths. [confidence: **high** — multiple sources: SCGA, Discover LA, Greenskeeper reviewers, GolfGreatly]. Source: multiple.
- **No water hazards** (per 2013 Richie's World; bunker count and hazard-type not changed by 2019 reno). [confidence: **medium**]. Source: https://www.richiesworldofgolf.com/
- **No driving range** — multiple reviewers confirm. [confidence: **high**]. Sources: Greenskeeper roarksown1 (02/10/25); GolfLink.
- **Greens (post-renovation):** "rolling truer than typical LA muni greens" (GolfGreatly, 2021). Reviewer consensus: best part of the course when maintained, but frequently bumpy / ball-marked. [confidence: **high**]. Sources: GolfGreatly; Greenskeeper roarksown1 (02/10/25); Greenskeeper dangerousbri (09/30/23) "slow greens and pretty flat. Lots of ball marks."
- **Green speed:** generally **medium** per multiple Greenskeeper reviewers (t8fish 07/2024, Itslikeimsayin 04/2024). [confidence: **high**]. Source: https://www.greenskeeper.org/southern_california/los_angeles/roosevelt_golf_course/reviews.cfm
- **Tee boxes are the weak point** — "lacking both adequate grass coverage and supple soil" (GolfGreatly); multiple Greenskeeper reviewers echo this. [confidence: **high**]. Sources: GolfGreatly; Greenskeeper roarksown1, t8fish, dangerousbri.
- **Grass types:** GolfPass lists greens = bent grass, fairways = bermuda grass. [confidence: **medium** — single source, possibly stale]. Source: https://www.golfpass.com/travel-advisor/courses/3833-roosevelt-municipal-golf-course (Note: bent grass on LA muni greens is atypical; may be poa annua in practice — one Greenskeeper reviewer mentions "poa annua greens" (Itslikeimsayin, 04/2024). **Conflict flagged.**)
- **Hardest four holes:** Holes **2, 4, 6, and 8** per Greenskeeper reviewer roarksown1 (stated twice across two reviews). Echoed by multiple reviewers talking about "probably four holes that are really challenging." [confidence: **high**]. Source: https://www.greenskeeper.org/southern_california/los_angeles/roosevelt_golf_course/reviews.cfm
- **Trees isolate each hole** — "each hole is isolated by trees" (per web-search summary of TripAdvisor/other coverage). [confidence: **medium**].
- **Pace of play:** Slow on weekends; 5-somes allowed; bookings go out every 10 minutes. [confidence: **high**]. Sources: multiple GolfPass and Greenskeeper reviewers. (Not strategic, but affects time-to-play.)
- Round time: 2:45 (mrgolf, Greenskeeper 08/2023) to nearly 18-hole on busy weekends. [confidence: **high**].

## Conflicts flagged

1. **Grass type — greens:** GolfPass says **bent grass**; Greenskeeper reviewer refers to **poa annua**. Bent grass is uncommon for LA munis; poa annua is more plausible. Flag for ground-truth before claiming.
2. **Hole 9 penalty-area description vs. Hole 6 penalty area:** The "rattlesnake habitat" penalty-area phrase appeared in web-search snippets attributed to *both* hole 6 (in the hole-6 search snippet) and hole 9 (in the explicit rattlesnake search). In the GolfGreatly WebFetch extract, the "rattlesnake habitat" is attached specifically to **hole 9**. Search-engine summaries appear to have conflated the two; hole 9 is the canonical location. [Resolution: trust the WebFetch extract over search snippets — hole 9.]
3. **Hole yardage — hole 3:** GolfLink and BlueGolf scorecard list 155 yd (black); Richie's World 2013 listed 156 yd. Trivial variance; current value = 155.
4. **Pre- vs post-renovation review:** Richie's World (2013) describes a bunker short of the 7th green ("green behind the bunker") — 2019 bunker rebuild may have altered this. Do not rely on Richie's for current bunker positions.
