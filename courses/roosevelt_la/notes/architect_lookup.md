# Roosevelt Golf Course — Architect & Course Metadata

**Course:** Roosevelt Golf Course (Roosevelt Municipal Golf Course)
**Location:** 2650 N. Vermont Avenue, Griffith Park, Los Angeles, CA 90027
**Coordinates:** 34°07'08"N, 118°17'38"W (≈ 34.1189, -118.2939 per Wikipedia) [1]
**Operator:** City of Los Angeles (Department of Recreation and Parks, Golf Division) [1][6]
**Compiled:** 2026-04-20

## Architect

**Original 1964 layout: uncertain — best available attribution is landscape architect John Ward, possibly with Billy Bell Jr.** [2][3]

- Forrest Richardson (the restoration architect) writes: "The existing layout was completed in 1964 by landscape architect John Ward, possibly with assistance from Billy Bell Jr." [2]
- Forrest Richardson's project page adds that the broader Griffith Park golf history includes Tom Bendelow's 1914 sand-green course and a William P. Bell rebuild, but attributes the current Roosevelt specifically to the "mid 1960s" replacement [3].
- Wikipedia does not name an architect [1].
- Treat the original-architect field as **Ward (attributed), possibly with Bell Jr. — not definitively confirmed by a primary City of Los Angeles source.**

**Restoration architect (2019): Forrest Richardson, ASGCA** — confirmed by Forrest Richardson Golf Course Architects [3][4] and Golf Course Architecture magazine [5].

## Year opened

**1964** — confirmed by Wikipedia ("Established 1964") [1] and by Forrest Richardson project notes ("existing layout was completed in 1964") [2][3]. The course was built in Vermont Canyon to replace an earlier Roosevelt Memorial Golf Course that was displaced by LA Zoo expansion [6].

## Renovation history

- **2019 restoration by Forrest Richardson (ASGCA) with builder Heritage Links.** Announced January 31, 2019; reopened after an 8-month project [3][4][5]. Budget ≈ $400,000 [3].
- Scope, per Forrest Richardson and Golf Course Architecture magazine [3][4][5]:
  - Bunker reconstruction using the "Better Billy Bunker" system; 5 greenside bunkers plus 1 cross bunker on the par-4 4th.
  - Greens enlarged to reclaim lost surface area.
  - New forward tees added; forward-tee yardage reduced by "more than 500 yards" to open the course to beginners.
  - New irrigation system sized for recycled (non-potable) water, which required multi-year environmental permitting.
  - Tree management — dead/dying trees removed, strategic replanting to screen roads and open city views; invasive species removed.
- No other major renovations between 1964 and 2019 are documented in the sources consulted.

## Grass types

- **Fairways:** Bermuda grass — per GolfPass [4] and Greenskeeper descriptive notes [7]. Confirmed by two independent third-party sources.
- **Greens:** Bentgrass — per GolfPass [4] and Greenskeeper descriptive notes [7]. Confirmed by two independent third-party sources.
- **Tees:** unknown — not specified in any consulted source. Common practice in Southern California municipal golf is bermuda tees, but that is not confirmed for Roosevelt.
- **Rough:** unknown — not specified in any consulted source. Richardson's notes mention "naturalized areas" at course edges [3], but do not identify the species of managed rough.

**Confidence note:** grass data comes from tourist/review sites (GolfPass, Greenskeeper), not from the LA City Golf operator or a superintendent interview, so "fairway = bermuda, greens = bent" should be re-verified against the operator or a superintendent before being treated as authoritative for Goose.

## Notable features / character

- **9-hole, par-33, walking-only executive-style course** [1][6][7].
- Set in Vermont Canyon directly below the Griffith Observatory and across N. Vermont Ave from the Greek Theatre [1][6][7].
- **Hilly terrain** — the course is routed through canyon topography; Forrest Richardson's own pull-quote is: "The course doesn't make you feel as if you've hiked the Alps, yet you become totally lost in the hills." [5]
- 2,496 yd from the back (Black) tees — shortest of the LA municipal 9-holers, but defended by terrain rather than length [3][5].
- Irrigated with **recycled water** post-2019 [3][4].
- Practice amenities: chipping and putting greens; **no driving range** [7].
- Operated by LA City Golf (same umbrella as Harding, Wilson, Rancho Park, etc.) [6].
